LeaveBy

Live

Know when to leave, not just when you have to be there.

A simple app with today’s events on the left and a Maps-style leave time on the right. Sample day: work, gym at Equinox, dinner at Bestia. You can switch walk, drive, or transit, ask the chat if the night still works, and try a phone reminder.
